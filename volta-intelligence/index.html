Flagship use case

A club loses its starting left-back. Here is what happens next.

The platform turns one verified event into a revenue-generating workflow: from detecting the squad gap to acting for the club under an authorised mandate.

Detect

A verified departure updates the squad graph. Source and confidence shown.

Assess

AI reviews available full-backs, the tactical system, replacement options and likely budget.

Identify

Locate the recruitment decision-maker and any previous relationship.

Approach

Draft a tailored introduction offering a mandate to source left-back profiles. A human approves before anything is sent.

Authorise

Record the club's agreement, scope and signatures. Run licensing and conflict checks.

Source

Match candidates by role, level, feasibility, wage and playing characteristics.

Present

Send a structured shortlist with video and financial parameters. Capture feedback.

Progress

Track negotiation, milestones, fee entitlements and mandate completion.

The platform

Twelve connected modules. One shared intelligence layer.

Global player intelligence

A living player passport: identity, eligibility, contract end-dates, video, analytics, representation status and source provenance.

Club intelligence and needs

Squad depth, expiring deals, decision-makers, tactical model and confirmed versus inferred recruitment briefs.

Agent and agency intelligence

Licensed agent directory, verified rosters, relationships, conflicts and lawful approach windows.

AI player-to-club matching

Bi-directional recommendations that explain fit, costs, constraints and uncertainty.

Transfer and contract radar

Contract thresholds at 3, 6 and 12 months, vacancies, staff changes and window alerts.

Relationship CRM

Football-specific institutional memory centred on people, not inboxes.

Outreach and presentations

Player dossiers, playlists, club-specific messages and multilingual sequences with human review.

Mandates and deal pipeline

Identified, approached, mandate pending, authorised, sourcing, shortlist, deal. E-signature and financial models.

Regulatory intelligence

FIFA and national rules, GBE and work permits, minors, training compensation and the 2027 transfer framework.

Scouting and development

Standardised templates, video tagging, longitudinal tracking and potential indicators.

Athlete management

Development plans, performance support, commercial opportunities and restricted welfare data.

Conversational AI assistant

Trust by design

Every recommendation shows its evidence and its uncertainty.

No participant gets unrestricted access. Players, clubs and agencies share only what their role, rights and verified relationships permit.

Confidence stateMeaning
ConfirmedOfficial source or direct verified confirmation
VerifiedVerified by an authorised party
ReportedThird-party information with a traceable origin
InferredModel-produced estimate or prediction
UnknownUnavailable, restricted or unverified

Representation rules

Licence, client identity, scope and written agreement before acting. Conflict and multiple-representation checks.

Privacy and permissions

Lawful sources, suppression lists, record-level permissions and stronger restrictions on youth, health and welfare data.

Minors

Guardian consent, approach rules and FIFA international transfer restrictions built into the workflow.

Human approval

External contact, publication and commitments always pass a human gate. AI output is never legal clearance.

Roadmap

Internal operating system first. Global platform second.

Phase 1: foundation

Player, club, agency and contact records; search; recruitment-need records; mandate pipeline; email, tasks and presentations; permissions and provenance.

Phase 2: intelligence

Matching, opportunity scores, contract and transfer radar, predicted club requirements, rules engine, financial modelling and the AI assistant.

Phase 3: expansion

Club and agent network with verified briefs, the Ghana youth pilot, pathway prediction, partner dashboards, APIs and multi-organisation subscriptions.

Phase boundaries are product decisions, not calendar promises. Scope, licensed data access and acceptance criteria are agreed with the development partner first.
