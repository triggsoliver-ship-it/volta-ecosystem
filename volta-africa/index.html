Three proposed products

One connected ecosystem for the player journey.

Africa scouting and recruitment tool

Match video, contextual metrics and player histories for search, comparison and shortlisting. For international scouts and club recruitment staff.

Player development tool

Feedback, development goals and profile building, with meaningful rewards for engagement and progress. For players, coaches, teams and academies.

Recruitment funnelling tool

A structured pathway from local identification and assessment to suitable club shortlists and opportunities. For Volta staff and overseas recruitment teams.

Ghana pilot

12 months. 300 players. Ages 12 to 16.

Partner clubs and schools, supported by local personnel, standardised assessment, video and consent-led data processes. Scale with evidence, not promises.

This is a youth development and evidence programme, not an under-18 international transfer programme. Safeguarding, appropriate consent, legitimate purpose, security and FIFA minor-transfer restrictions apply throughout.

Set up the system

Partner agreements, safeguarding, guardian permissions, data specification and scout training.

Capture evidence

Repeatable video, match data and position-specific scouting.

Build intelligence

Longitudinal profiles, quality controls and comparable clips.

Test the value

Measure repeatability, completeness and scouting usefulness.

Open verified club access

Permissioned scouting views and compliant introductions, only once the evidence is credible.
